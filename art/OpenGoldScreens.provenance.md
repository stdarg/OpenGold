# OpenGold screen review assets

These two static review images were generated with the built-in image generation
tool from user-supplied `art/TitleScreen.png`. They are not integrated into the game.
The user explicitly requested matching screens with that image's background.
The original files are unchanged.

- `OpenGoldSplash.png`: single-line OpenGold lettering.
- `OpenGoldTitle.png`: larger, stacked OPEN / GOLD lettering.

The source background's authorship and distribution rights have not been verified.
Removing franchise branding does not establish clearance for the retained artwork.
AI editing preserved the composition but introduced small texture differences.
