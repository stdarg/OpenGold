# Levels 5–10 plan

Raising the level cap from 4 to 10 ([#176](https://github.com/stdarg/OpenGold/issues/176)),
toward the level-15 cap of [SCOPE-1](SRD-DECISIONS.md#scope-1-2026-09-30-level-cap-deferrals-and-rare-situations).
The facts (XP, slots, every class and subclass feature at levels 5–10, and all
114 spells of levels 3–5 with a proposed triage) are in the
[inventory](LEVELS-5-10-INVENTORY.md).

## How the work runs

As for levels 1–4 ([CLASS-1](SRD-DECISIONS.md#class-1-2026-10-04-finishing-levels-14-for-all-classes)):
one class at a time, each finished through level 10 before the next, committed
and pushed when its tests pass. A class's cap rises to 10 only when its
features work; until then it stays at 4, so no character reaches a level with
missing features.

1. **Shared progression** (no class's cap rises yet): XP to 64,000;
   Proficiency Bonus +3 at 5 and +4 at 9 everywhere it is used; Hit Points per
   level; spell slots of levels 3–5 and Pact slots of levels 3–5; casting the
   existing spells with those slots (the "waits for level-three slots" notes);
   cantrip upgrades at level 5; the Ability Score Improvement or feat at 8
   (Fighter also 6, Rogue also 10).
2. **Extra Attack**, shared by Barbarian, Fighter, Monk, Paladin and Ranger,
   with Weapon Mastery, Cleave, Sneak Attack and the other once-per-turn rules.
3. **Martial classes:** Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Monk, Paladin, Ranger.
   Paladin brings auras (Aura of Protection, Devotion, Courage), the first
   effects that move with a creature.
4. **Casters:** Cleric, Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Bard, Druid. Each brings
   the kept spells it first needs. New systems arrive with the first class that
   needs them:
   - Cleric: Revivify and Raise Dead (resurrection, #175), Spirit Guardians
     (an area that moves with its caster), Death Ward.
   - Wizard: Fireball and the other area spells, Dispel Magic and Counterspell
     (a record of spells active on each creature), walls (Wall of Fire, Wall of
     Force), Haste and Slow.
   - Druid: Giant Insect (the first summoned ally, #173), the level-8 Wild
     Shape forms.

New controls follow the existing patterns (the A cycle, React/Decline, the
Training and Spell Choices pages, the Camp dialog's Cast / Use list). Anything
that does not fit is asked about with screenshots first.
