OpenGold combat sprite specification: bipedal-v1 (2026-10-17)

Create one independently designed full-body monster combat sprite in crisp pixel
art. Use the monster's current text description and the selected equipment set.
No original game image, extracted sprite, or original-art contact sheet may be an
input reference. Do not copy the pose-guide mannequin's identity or clothes.

FACING: Front three-quarter view toward the RIGHT EDGE OF THE IMAGE (screen
right), approximately 45 degrees between frontal and right-side profile. The
chest/front of the body remains partly visible. Keep head, torso and feet oriented
consistently. Preserve this view in BOTH frames; do not turn away into a rear view
or full side profile during the action. The attack travels toward screen right.

MELEE READY: Hold the one-handed or two-handed weapon UP in a combat guard,
poised to strike. It must not dangle at rest. One-handed weapons have one hand on
the grip; two-handed weapons have both hands visibly and plausibly on the grip
or shaft. A thrusting weapon can be held up and aimed forward, ready to thrust.
MELEE ACTION: Show the weapon near the END of a strike: a late swing or a nearly
completed thrust appropriate to the weapon. Show a clear change from ready, not
another guard or windup. Maintain the appropriate grip through the strike.

PROJECTILE READY: Hold the weapon loaded and ready to fire: arrow nocked on bow,
bolt loaded on crossbow, or stone seated in sling pouch. This is before release.
PROJECTILE ACTION: The instant just AFTER the arrow, bolt, stone or other
projectile has been released toward screen right, with appropriate follow-through.
The projectile is no longer nocked, loaded or in the sling pouch; it may already
be outside the image. Keep the bow, crossbow or sling in the character's hands.
For a thrown weapon, ready holds it poised to throw; action is just after release
and that thrown weapon is no longer in the hand. Do not turn a wand into a bow.
If an unarmed, magical or unusual attack is not specified by the description,
ask the user for its ready/action behavior before generating it.

EQUIPMENT: Draw only the selected weapon/shield setup, plus appropriate other
gear from the record. Retain the shield in both frames when the set includes it.
Do not add a shield to a two-handed or ranged setup. Preserve the character's
identity, clothing, colors, handedness and equipment across the pair and all its
weapon sets. Treat conflicting descriptions/equipment as a question to resolve,
not permission to invent a different setup.

WEAPON-SET COVERAGE (generation workflow): Inspect the monster's entire current
equipment list and enumerate every weapon set, including weapons not currently
readied. Each set needs its own ready.png AND action.png. A one-handed weapon
with an available shield is one unit; without a shield it is a weapon-only set.
Each ranged weapon and each two-handed weapon is a separate set. Bow + mace +
shield therefore needs a bow pair and a mace/shield pair. Bow + greatsword needs
a bow pair and a greatsword pair. Ask the user if a set or equipment classification
is unclear. This image request depicts ONLY its selected set and specified pose;
create the other sets and poses in separate requests, not a combined sheet.

PAIR REFERENCES: The approved original pose guide controls camera and pose.
The approved new ready sprite controls this monster's identity, palette, scale
and foot baseline when generating action. For another weapon set, also reference
this monster's first approved NEW ready sprite to retain identity. Reference
images must be supplied with the request, not merely named in its text.

SHARED FRAME BUDGET: Plan BOTH poses before drawing either one. Identify the
pose with the widest or tallest full-body-and-weapon silhouette, reserve a
transparent margin around that larger pose, and use its camera distance for
both images. The character's head, torso, limbs and equipment keep the same
apparent pixel scale in ready and action. Do not shrink the whole action figure
to fit a wider attack, and do not enlarge the ready figure to fill spare space.
Change the pose within the shared canvas; if the attack cannot fit at the
chosen body scale, remake the pair at a smaller common scale.

OUTPUT: A single full-body figure, genuine transparent background, no painted
checkerboard, ground plane, labels, borders, scenery, shadows extending off the
figure or motion blur. Keep body and equipment entirely inside the frame with
transparent margin. Final assets are 128 x 128 PNGs with alpha. Larger working
images are allowed; export with nearest-neighbor sampling and preserve alpha.
Keep camera, character scale and grounded foot baseline consistent across poses
and weapon sets; do not resize each pose independently to fill the canvas. The
action must read clearly at normal combat size, not only in a magnified preview.
